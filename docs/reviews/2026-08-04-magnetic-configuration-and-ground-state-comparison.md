# Scientific review — Magnetic Configuration and Ground-State Comparison

Reviewed: 2026-08-04

The topic is **reviewed within the declared educational and execution scope**. It distinguishes an SCF solution from the lowest member of an enumerated magnetic candidate set, net moment from local magnetic order, a fixed-geometry electronic comparison from a relaxed magnetostructural comparison, and a DFT ordering difference from a finite-temperature or experimental magnetic claim.

The companion script uses invented compatible candidate energies and final moment labels. Execution success is not magnetic convergence, ground-state completeness, an exchange measurement, a transition-temperature prediction, or a scientific conclusion. The media are original conceptual diagrams of invented data.
