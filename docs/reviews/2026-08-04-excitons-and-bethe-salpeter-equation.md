# Scientific review — Excitons and the Bethe--Salpeter Equation

Reviewed 2026-08-04. The overview distinguishes a quasiparticle continuum from neutral electron--hole excitations, identifies the direct and exchange kernel terms, defines the compatibility conditions behind a binding-energy subtraction, and separates optical brightness, numerical convergence, and experimental comparison. It is **reviewed within the declared educational scope**.

No companion execution or material image is introduced for this topic. The overview does not claim that a BSE solver has run, that an exciton is converged, that a lifetime has been calculated, or that a material conclusion follows.
