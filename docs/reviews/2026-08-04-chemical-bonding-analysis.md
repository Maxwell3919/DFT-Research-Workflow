# Scientific review — Chemical Bonding Analysis

Reviewed: 2026-08-04

The topic is **reviewed within the declared educational and execution scope**. It distinguishes a total-energy comparison from a representation-dependent bonding indicator, COHP/COOP projection quality from a pair-curve interpretation, and ELF or density topology from a unique bond order or stability claim.

The companion script uses invented energy-resolved pair contributions. Execution success is not a projection-quality result, DFT convergence, bond-strength measurement, material-stability result, or a scientific conclusion. The media are original conceptual diagrams of invented data.

The primary COHP method is https://doi.org/10.1021/j100135a014 and its plane-wave projection boundary is https://doi.org/10.1021/jp202489s. Localization and topology boundaries are supported by https://doi.org/10.1063/1.458517, https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.199718081, https://doi.org/10.1007/s002140000233, and https://schmeling.ac.rwth-aachen.de/cohp/.
