## Acceptance use

For a representative page, the reviewer must be able to answer: what object is needed, where it comes from, what to click or type, what output should appear, what to inspect, what the quickest useful check does and does not prove, how to respond to failure, and where to continue. A page that only communicates “rigorous validation is required” fails this matrix.
